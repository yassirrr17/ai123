100-Prospect Sales Script

Internal playbook for cold outreach to local NZ businesses. Use these scripts to systematically pitch the ReviewFlow AI value proposition.

1

Opening & Permission

Goal: Get past the gatekeeper and secure 30 seconds of attention.

"Hi, is the owner or manager available?

Hi [Name], my name is [Your Name]. I know you're busy running [Business Name], so I'll be brief. I'm working on a small software product for local businesses that automatically follows up with customers after they've completed a service.

Do you have 30 seconds for me to explain how it works?"

2

The 'Why' & The Problem

"I noticed you guys do great work, but looking at your Google Reviews, it seems like maybe not every happy customer is leaving one. Usually, that's just because business owners don't have the time to manually text everyone asking for a review."

3

The Value Prop & Ask

"We've built a system that connects to your booking calendar or POS. When a customer finishes a job, it automatically sends them a friendly text asking for a review. It filters out bad feedback internally and pushes the 5-star reviews straight to Google.

Would you be open to a quick 5-minute demo next week to see if it could help boost your local ranking?"

shield Objection Handling

"We already get enough business."

"That's great! More reviews actually act as a moat against competitors moving into your area, and helps you rank higher organically so you don't have to pay for ads."

"I don't have time to set this up."

"I completely understand. That's why we do the full setup for you. It takes about 10 minutes of your time on a call, and then it runs completely in the background."

"Send me an email."

"Will do. What's the best email address? I'll send over a 1-page summary. If you like what you see, we can chat later next week." (Move to Email Sequence)

Key Tone Directives

  • Speak slowly and clearly.
  • Sound like a local peer, not a corporate robot.
  • Emphasize "automatic" and "background".
